Document camera configuration

Adjust the SmartCapture Document settings below, then launch the camera. Anything left untouched uses the SDK default.

Camera screen

By disabling the show back button setting, the back button will not be shown on the document camera screen. Default: enabled

By enabling the hide help icon setting, the help icon will not appear on the document camera. Default: disabled

By enabling the show preview screen setting, the user reviews the captured image on a review/retake screen before it is returned. Note this changes when the capture and close events fire. Default: disabled

The amount of seconds entered will reflect how long the "photo taken" overlay is shown before the preview screen appears. Default: 1.5 seconds

Capture mode

Enabling both auto-capture and tap to capture, the document camera will initialise with auto-capture running, and after the auto-capture delay toggle time is met, the auto-capture toggle will appear. Enabling auto-capture and disabling tap to capture, the document camera will only have the option for auto-capture. Disabling auto-capture and enabling tap to capture, the document camera will initialise straight into the tap to capture mode, with the ability to switch to auto-capture.

Auto-capture is the launch mode and the auto-capture timeout is armed. Default: enabled
